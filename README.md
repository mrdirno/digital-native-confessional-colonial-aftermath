## Entry Points for Contributors

**Start anywhere. Fork freely. No permission needed.**

### Story Entry Points

- Write a scene where a character discovers a direct digital link (like server routing) between their modern life and a historical colonial institution.
- Create a monologue from the perspective of an elder who refuses to have their story recorded, explaining why memory must be embodied, not stored.
- Draft a social media post from Zuri that she writes but ultimately deletes—one that is too messy, too real, and unfit for her curated brand.
- Imagine a conversation between Baraka and the Lamu fisherman, meeting by chance, discussing what was taken from them and what remains.

### Technical Entry Points

- Build a simple web tool that visually maps the physical locations of data centers owned by major tech corporations onto historical maps of colonial trade routes.
- Create a 'data dignity' license—a mock legal framework for a story that outlines terms for ethical use of personal cultural data and narrative.
